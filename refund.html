Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

14-Day 100% Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind the quality of our AI-powered courses and mentorship. If you are not satisfied within 14 days of enrollment, you are entitled to a full refund, no awkward questions asked.

1. Eligibility for Refund

A full refund request is accepted if:

  • The refund request is initiated within 14 days of the original payment timestamp.
  • Less than 30% of the program's video lectures or assignments have been completed or marked as certified.
  • No official completion certificate has been issued or verified for that specific program.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a cancellation, send an email to support@techindro.com with your:

  • Registered mobile number / student email
  • Course Name / ID
  • Payment Transaction ID (or Hyperswitch/UPI reference number)

3. Processing Timeline

Once approved, your refund will be processed back to the original payment source (UPI account, Credit/Debit card, or Net Banking) within 5 to 7 working days, in accordance with standard RBI banking guidelines.

4. Course Transfers & Batch Changes

If you cannot continue with your current cohort, you can request a free batch transfer to a future cohort within 6 months of enrollment by contacting our mentorship coordinators.
